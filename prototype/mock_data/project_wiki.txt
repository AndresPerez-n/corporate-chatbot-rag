=== ENGINEERING PROCESSES ===

Sprint cadence: 2-week sprints. Planning Monday AM, retro Friday PM.
All teams use Jira. Epics require a product brief in Confluence before work starts.

Code review policy:
  All PRs require at least 1 approval (2 for auth, payments, or data pipeline changes)
  PRs open > 3 days without review: ping author in #eng-reviews
  Automated checks must pass before merge (CI, linting, type checks)
  Squash merge to main preferred

Architecture Decision Records (ADRs):
  New tech choices or significant architectural changes require an ADR.
  Template: Confluence > Engineering > ADR Template
  Must be approved by 2 staff engineers before implementation begins.
